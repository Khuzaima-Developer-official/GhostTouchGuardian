# Privacy Policy — Ghost Touch Guardian

**Last updated:** November 2026  
**Developer:** Khuzaima Developer  
**App:** Ghost Touch Guardian  
**Package:** `com.antigravity.ghosttouchguardian`

---

## 1. Data We Collect

Ghost Touch Guardian collects **no personal data**.

The app processes touch events **locally on your device only**. No data leaves your device.

| What | Collected? | Sent anywhere? |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Touch coordinates | Processed in RAM | Never |
| Touch timestamps | Processed in RAM | Never |
| Device identifiers | Never | Never |
| Location | Never | Never |
| Personal information | Never | Never |

---

## 2. Local Storage Only

The app stores the following **only on your device** via Android SharedPreferences:

- Selected protection level (Light / Balanced / High)
- Service enabled/disabled state

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

---

## 3. Permissions Used

| Permission | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` | Run background ghost touch detection |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | Auto-start protection after reboot |
| `ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE` | Intercept and suppress ghost touch events |

No internet permission is requested or used.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

Ghost Touch Guardian uses **no third-party SDKs, analytics, or advertising** services.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.
