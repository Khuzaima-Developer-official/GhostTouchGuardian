Experiencing frustrating phantom touches, erratic cursor jumps, or trailing clicks after lifting your finger?

**Ghost Touch Guardian** is a lightweight, ultra-high-efficiency utility engineered to detect, analyze, and suppress capacitive digitizer anomalies, static sparks, and post-touch release echoes on all Android smartphones and tablets.

---

#### 🌟 Key Features & Capabilities:

- **Unified Ghost Touch Test Lab (Ghost-Proof Screen)**
  A dedicated testing arena completely locked against accidental ghost clicks. Displays live dwell time, touch area, and coordinate jitter for every event. Exit is strictly guarded by a human-finger verification slider (displacement, contact size, and dwell time verified) so random phantom touches can never exit or disrupt testing.

- **3 Streamlined Protection Levels (Set from Dashboard)**
  - **Light (Gaming):** Minimum dwell filtering (22ms) for high-APM competitive gamers who need maximum responsiveness with zero dropped inputs.
  - **Balanced (Default):** Optimal threshold (35ms dwell / 55ms echo) for everyday social, browsing, and messaging use.
  - **High (Damaged Screen):** Heavy-duty suppression (55ms dwell / 75ms echo) designed for cracked digitizers, water-damaged screens, or UV screen protector glue buildup.

- **Background Auto-Shield (Runs Even When App is Closed)**
  Runs as an ultra-efficient foreground service with accessibility support, automatically intercepting and nullifying ghost taps system-wide across all your apps and games.

- **Universal Android & Full 64-Bit Architecture**
  Engineered for modern 64-bit devices (Google Pixel 7/8/9, Galaxy S23/S24, OnePlus, Xiaomi) with full `arm64-v8a` optimization while maintaining backward compatibility with `armeabi-v7a` and `x86_64`.

- **Zero-Latency S Pen & Stylus Bypass**
  Drawing, sketching, or taking notes with Samsung S Pen or active styluses? The guardian instantly detects `TOOL_TYPE_STYLUS` and disengages all temporal filters, giving you full native 120Hz/240Hz responsiveness.

- **Hardware Touch Recalibration**
  - **Samsung Devices:** Direct 1-tap launcher to Samsung's secret `*#2663#` TSP firmware menu to reset capacitive baseline matrices.
  - **Universal Devices:** Instant access to system display touch calibration.

- **Microscopic Memory Footprint (~3.6 Kilobytes)**
  Uses flat primitive circular ring buffers (`LongArray`, `FloatArray`). Zero garbage collection overhead, zero memory leaks, and mathematically bounded RAM usage.

- **0.00% Screen-Off Battery Drain**
  Equipped with a hardware-level `ScreenStateReceiver` that completely freezes all monitoring loops the moment your screen turns off. No wake locks, no battery drain.
